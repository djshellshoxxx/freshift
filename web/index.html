Discovered hosts join the normal crawl queue within the scan limits. For multi-tenant roots such as github.io, results may belong to unrelated users.
DNS & reverse-IP researchALL OPTIONAL
PTR records identify configured DNS hostnames, not all websites sharing an IP. Forward-confirmed PTR names are added as new seeds (subject to scope filters, robots rules and scan limits). DNS-resolved IPs may differ from the HTTP connection's actual peer, especially behind CDNs. In Pages, DNS lookups require DNS-over-HTTPS access.
Scan limits & crawl controls ⌄

Rechecks discovery providers and revisits known URLs each round. Limits apply per round. The Go engine continues with its browser closed and restores active perpetual scans on restart. Standalone Pages scans only while its tab stays open; for unattended scans, connect Pages to an always-on Go engine.

Target filters ⌄
A TLD filter excludes IP-address targets; leave it blank to include direct IPs.
